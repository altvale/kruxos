# Signing In to Your KruxOS Account

By the end of this page, you'll know how magic-link sign-in works on [kruxos.com](https://kruxos.com) and what to do when a link doesn't arrive.

## How sign-in works

Your KruxOS account has no password. Signing in (and signing up) uses a **magic link**:

1. Enter your email address at [kruxos.com/signup](https://kruxos.com/signup) (or on the [activation page](https://kruxos.com/activate)).
2. We email you a sign-in link.
3. Open the link — you're signed in, and your account is created automatically the first time.

!!! note "Links are single-use and short-lived"
    Each link **works once** and **expires 15 minutes** after it's sent. If it's
    expired or already used, just request a new one — there's no limit.

If you started from the activation page, open the link **on the same device** you're activating from so the flow can continue where you left off.

## Didn't get the email?

- **Check your spam or junk folder.** Automated sign-in emails are occasionally filtered.
- **Check the address.** The email goes exactly to what you typed — a typo means it lands somewhere else (or nowhere). Re-enter your email and request a new link.
- **Wait a minute, then retry.** Delivery is usually seconds, but a busy mail provider can lag. Requesting a new link invalidates nothing — the newest link simply works.
- **Corporate mail filters.** If your organisation quarantines external mail, ask your admin to allow mail from kruxos.com, or use a personal address for your account.

## Still stuck?

[Contact support](https://kruxos.com/contact/) and include the email address you're trying to sign in with.
